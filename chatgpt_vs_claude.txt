ChatGPT vs Claude

ChatGPT and Claude are both AI assistants, but they can feel different in use.

ChatGPT
- Often good for brainstorming, coding help, summaries, and quick answers.
- Can be more conversational and flexible in style.
- Works well for drafting content and turning rough ideas into structured text.

Claude
- Often strong at reading and reasoning over long text.
- Can be useful for careful writing, document analysis, and longer context tasks.
- Tends to feel calm and detailed in its responses.

Quick comparison
- ChatGPT: fast, versatile, and broad.
- Claude: thoughtful, long-context friendly, and detail-oriented.

Which one is better depends on the task.
If you want speed and variety, ChatGPT can be a strong choice.
If you want deep reading and long-form analysis, Claude can be a strong choice.
