ChatGPT vs Claude

ChatGPT and Claude are both AI assistants designed to understand prompts, answer questions,
help with writing, explain technical topics, and assist with coding tasks. They are similar
in that they use large language models and work best when the user gives clear context and
specific instructions.

ChatGPT is made by OpenAI. It is often used for brainstorming, programming help, tutoring,
summarization, and building workflows with OpenAI tools and APIs. Claude is made by Anthropic.
It is also used for writing, analysis, coding support, document review, and conversational
assistance.

The better choice depends on the task, the available tools, model versions, privacy needs,
cost, and personal preference. For many everyday tasks, both can be useful. The most practical
approach is to test each assistant with the same real task and compare the quality, accuracy,
speed, and style of the results.
